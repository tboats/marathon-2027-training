# LA Marathon 2027 & Vancouver Marathon Coaching Guidelines

> Project-level developer instructions, coaching mandates, and evaluation standards.

## 1. Quality & Interval Workout Reporting Mandate (MANDATORY)

For **all interval and quality sessions** (Cruise Intervals, Threshold Reps, Tempo Intervals, Strides/Surges, Hill Repeats, Time Trials, Benchmark Races):

Whenever the user completes an interval workout or asks for an update, the Coach / AI Agent **MUST** provide a detailed **Rep-by-Rep Interval Evaluation** containing:

1. **Rep-by-Rep Performance Table**:
   - **Rep # & Prescribed vs Actual Distance**
   - **Time & Raw Pace** (mm:ss / mi)
   - **Heart Rate**: Average HR and Peak HR per rep
   - **Biomechanics**: Cadence (Avg & Peak spm), Ground Contact Time (ms), and Vertical Ratio (%)
   - **Power**: Average running power (W) if available
   - **Composite Average**: Summary line showing overall pace, HR, and mechanics across all quality reps

2. **Recovery Jog Telemetry**:
   - Recovery interval duration (mm:ss) and distance (miles) between each rep
   - Heart rate recovery kinetics: Peak HR at the end of the rep down to low HR at the end of the recovery jog
   - Recovery clearance rate assessment (metabolic shuttle efficiency)

3. **Physiological & Strategic Insights**:
   - **Pacing Precision**: Split consistency (1st half vs 2nd half of rep) and adherence to prescribed velocity
   - **Effort Modulation**: Surrendering pace on uphill segments to maintain target HR ceiling vs pushing on flats
   - **Cardiac Drift**: Comparison of HR between early reps vs late reps at identical pace
   - **Biomechanical Degradation**: Tracking whether GCT lengthened or cadence dropped as fatigue accumulated

## 2. Zero-Double-Days & Lifting Mandate

- **Tuesdays, Fridays, Saturdays, Sundays**: Strictly **RUN ONLY — ZERO LIFTING**.
- **Mondays**: Non-running day dedicated to Bi-Weekly Heavy Leg Resistance Strength (Trap bar deadlifts, Bulgarian split squats, step-ups, heavy calf raises). Low volume (3-5 reps), high load. Strictly zero running.
- **Thursdays**: Non-running day dedicated to Upper Body & Core Stability. Zero heavy leg loading.
- **Wednesdays**: Midweek Aerobic Anchor (Run Only).

## 3. Pacing & HR Zone Ceilings

- **Zone 1 / Recovery**: < 135 bpm (strict ceiling ~138 bpm). Conversational recovery jog (9:15–9:45/mi).
- **Zone 2 / Aerobic Base**: 136–145 bpm (strict 146 bpm ceiling on long runs). Steady continuous cruise (8:30–9:00/mi).
- **Zone 3 / Marathon Pace**: 151–156 bpm. Target MP 7:26/mi (3:15:00 goal).
- **Zone 4 / Lactate Threshold**: 158–164 bpm. Cruise intervals & T-pace (7:00–7:10/mi).
- **Zone 5 / VO2max & Benchmark**: > 168 bpm. All-out time trials & race finishes.
